# Product Success and Quality in Use

## Product Success Contract

A product milestone, repeated-use claim, daily-driver claim, or release claim
requires initiation-time Product Success Contract authority.

The contract names claim id, product surface, actor, job, context, desired
outcome, critical journey, first value event, relevant quality dimensions,
proof tier, forbidden substitutions, review owner, and claim ceiling.

A focused lane inherits the current milestone contract; it does not create its
own product-success contract or receipt. Planning authorizes work but cannot
prove the product claim. Stale or mismatched evidence lowers only the claim
that consumes it.

## Product Fitness

Product Fitness asks whether the product helps the actor in context.

- Distinguish observed, inferred, assumed, and missing facts.
- Select quality dimensions because the current job depends on them; do not
  expand every claim into a universal audit.
- A single representative journey may prove first useful value only. Adoption
  and continuance need repeated current use.
- Evaluate accessibility, cognitive load, recovery burden, trust burden, and
  human attention when material to the current surface and actor.
- Memory, screenshots, old packets, receipts, install success, unit tests, and
  reviewer agreement cannot substitute for same-surface use.

## Product Cohesion

Product Cohesion asks whether the journey makes sense end to end.

The journey binds actor, steps, interaction boundaries, first value event,
failure path, recovery path, and same-surface requirement. Source checks,
package publication, discovery, smoke tests, fixtures, Cohesion, or Fitness
alone do not prove product success.

Persist one concise milestone outcome only when a current claim, custody
handoff, recovery need, or release consumes it. Ordinary observations remain
ephemeral or in the active plan.

## Review ownership

The active plan names the Product/Simplicity reviewer or product owner. Review
checks contract lineage, forbidden substitutions, same-surface journey,
failure/recovery, and claim ceiling. Broader release or Fitness records are
required only for the corresponding later claim.
