# ExecPlan Lane

Use this skill to create a lane contract. A lane is a large, coherent chunk of goal work with isolated ownership and proof obligations.

## Lane Size Rule

Prefer the largest non-overlapping lane that is safe to run independently. Do not create tiny, small, or medium lanes when the work can be assigned as one coherent ExecPlan.

Avoid mega-lanes that block parallelism or mix incompatible ownership.

## Required Sections

Every lane ExecPlan must include:

- purpose and observable outcome;
- progress;
- surprises and discoveries;
- decision log;
- outcomes and retrospective;
- context and orientation;
- owned paths;
- forbidden/shared paths;
- dependencies and consumed claims;
- concrete work;
- exact commands;
- validation and acceptance;
- live beneficial end-to-end proof;
- idempotence and recovery;
- artifact paths;
- ready receipt path;
- claim ceiling.
- parent-thread completion message contract.

## Launch Gate

A lane cannot launch until:

- the ExecPlan is self-contained;
- the lane has a worktree or equivalent isolated workspace;
- owned paths do not overlap active lanes without an explicit lock;
- forbidden paths are named;
- the proof surface is strong enough for the lane's claims;
- the live beneficial e2e task is defined;
- lane-owner model and reasoning are recorded; future Codex app worktree lane
  owners default to `gpt-5.5` with `low` reasoning unless the ExecPlan names a
  concrete risk that justifies more;
- required review team, model, and reasoning are specified when the lane is
  material;
- parent monitoring mode is recorded.
  If the parent will use idle heartbeat orchestration, the lane's ready message
  and artifacts must populate the automation evidence cursors: goal/contract,
  lane/session, repo/artifact, automation/thread, and changed receipts.

## Completion Gate

A lane cannot close until:

- progress and outcomes reflect reality;
- command evidence exists;
- evidence paths exist;
- worktree is clean or explicit uncommitted preservation is recorded;
- stale scratch is removed or classified;
- claim ceiling is honest;
- ready receipt is generated by the validator;
- final parent-thread message includes lane id, branch, worktree, commit,
  changed paths, commands and exit codes, artifacts, ready receipt, claim
  ceiling, worktree status, blockers, and recommended parent action;
- teardown condition is met.

## Review Cadence

Material lane review uses one fresh four-persona sign-off round after fresh
validator, review-target, and archive receipts are generated. The required
personas are contract and claim falsifier, orchestration and recovery
falsifier, security trust-boundary falsifier, and product and simplicity
falsifier.

All material reviewers use `gpt-5.5` with `high` reasoning, receive the full
current review scope, and return `SIGN_OFF` only when no material blocker
remains against the same current anchors. Any `REVISE_BEFORE_NEXT_PHASE` or
`BLOCKED` verdict invalidates the round; repair the blockers, regenerate
anchors, close the reviewers, and launch a fresh full-scope round.

Do not copy reviewer settings onto lane owners. Worktree lane owners default to
`gpt-5.5` with `low` reasoning for cost control.
