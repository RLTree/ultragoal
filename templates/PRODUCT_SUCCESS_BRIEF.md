before planning. The Product Success Contract remains normative; this brief is
a typed read-only inception input and cannot promote a claim.

Use `harness-ultragoal.product-success-brief.v2` for active evidence-led
sequencing. Bind the current Product Success Contract ID and digest, exact claim
IDs, target problem, audience, job, context, desired outcome, first value event,
operator, opaque real-repository/task identity, `PS-ENTRY` public surface,
forbidden bypasses, protected invariants, ordered first truth loop, one causal
failure/recovery control, preservation and repeat-use expectations, parked depth
triggers, evidence class, evidence ladder, and claim ceiling.

or satisfy Product Fitness after AMEND-004.
