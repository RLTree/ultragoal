# Product Eval Protocol

Required fields: eval scenarios, red paths, green paths, negative paths, first-value path, time-to-value path, adoption loop, continuance signal, behavior-level evaluation cases (baseline, negative, overlap, already-specified, no-change, held-out, semantic mutation, and specification evolution), calibrated repair budget, failure and recovery burden, and claim ceiling.

Behavior-level evaluation must grade activation, instruction following, authority composition, verified outcome, trajectory and recovery, no-change judgment, effect and security compliance, evidence honesty, supervisory burden, and cost/latency. Repair budgets name the task family and operating envelope, state the evidence used to calibrate the attempt count, and reject universal counts.
